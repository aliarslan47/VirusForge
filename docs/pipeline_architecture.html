VirusForge Pipeline DAG

The real run() data-dependency graph. Shared trunk V00→V04; V04 (geNomad) is the molecule decision — DNA/phage and RNA virus branches split and rejoin at the V12 report. V02/V03/V04/V06 are single modules that fork internally by molecule. Read type (short/long/hybrid/assembly) is a separate, orthogonal axis.
